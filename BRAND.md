# CISTYR Brand Direction

**Tagline:** Called in Sick, To Your Rescue.

**Positioning:** The fastest, most direct way for businesses in fast food, retail, and medical support to get reliable same-day or next-day coverage when someone calls in sick — and for workers to pick up extra shifts instantly with no middlemen.

---

## Logo

**Primary Logo:** Horizontal lockup (see `assets/logo.jpg`)
- Use this version in navbars, headers, and most marketing.
- Always keep clear space around it equal to the height of the "CISTYR" text.

**Icon / App Icon:** Rounded square version (`assets/icon.jpg`)
- Use for favicon, PWA, mobile app icon, social avatars.

**Usage Rules:**
- Do not stretch or distort.
- Minimum width on web: ~120px for the horizontal version.
- On dark backgrounds, use a white or light version (request if needed).
- Never add effects, shadows, or outlines unless approved.

---

## Color Palette

**Primary (Help / Trust / Go)**
- Emerald-600: `#059669` (main brand green)
- Emerald-700: `#047857`

**Accent (Urgent / Called-in-Sick)**
- Red-600: `#dc2626` or `#ef4444`
- Use sparingly for alerts, "To Your Rescue" buttons, and open shift status.

**Neutrals**
- Slate-900 / almost black for headlines
- Slate-600 for body text
- Slate-100 / white for cards and backgrounds

**Supporting**
- Teal accents for variety
- Warm orange for Fast Food category
- Blue for Retail
- Purple for Medical Non-Essential

---

## Typography

**Headlines & Logo-adjacent:** Poppins (700 weight) — already used in `.logo-font`

**Body & UI:** Inter (400–600 weights) — clean, highly legible on mobile and desktop.

**Hierarchy:**
- H1:  text-6xl / text-7xl, tracking-tighter, bold
- Section headers: text-4xl, font-bold, tracking-tight
- Body: text-base / text-sm

---

## Visual Style & Photography / Illustration

**Overall Vibe:** Modern, optimistic, reliable, fast, human.
- Red-to-green transition is the hero visual motif (problem → solution).
- Clean, slightly rounded corners (rounded-3xl in UI).
- Generous whitespace.
- Confident but approachable people (workers who show up and deliver).

**Illustration Style:** Flat + subtle gradients, optimistic color, diverse representation (matches the hero visual).

**Photography Direction (when adding real photos later):**
- Real workers and managers in real California locations (fast food counters, retail floors, clinic front desks).
- Natural light, genuine moments of connection/handshake/arrival.
- Avoid stocky or overly corporate looks.

---

## Voice & Messaging

**Tone:** Direct • Reliable • Urgent but optimistic • No BS

**Key Phrases:**
- "Called in Sick, To Your Rescue."
- "Same-day coverage when plans change."
- "Direct matching. No middlemen."
- "Green light = covered."

**Do's:**
- Emphasize speed and direct connection.
- Celebrate the people who show up.
- Be transparent about being a matching platform (compliance-friendly).

**Don'ts:**
- Don't sound like a traditional staffing agency ("we provide qualified temps...").
- Avoid corporate jargon or slow-sounding language.
- Don't overpromise on background checks or guarantees (users handle verification).
